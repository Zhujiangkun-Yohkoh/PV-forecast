# Literature sources and novelty check (2023–2026)

Only publisher, conference, DOI, arXiv, or official project pages were retained. Searches were performed in August 2026; “not found” means not found in this bounded review, not proof of absence.

| Year | Work | Venue/task | Mechanism and relevance | Overlap / threat | Primary source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | A cascaded deep learning framework for photovoltaic power forecasting with multi-fidelity inputs | Energy; day-ahead PV | Cascades observation and NWP information of different fidelity | Strong threat to generic multi-rate/cascade claims; different from causal past-only H12 trajectory decoding | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2023.126636 |
| 2023 | Short-Term Photovoltaic Power Forecasting Based on a Novel Autoformer Model | Symmetry; short-term PV | De-stationary attention and a multi-scale framework on 5-min/1-h data | Generic multi-scale PV forecasting is already published | https://doi.org/10.3390/sym15010238 |
| 2023 | Development of forecasting method of time variation of net solar output over wide area using grand data based neural network | Solar Compass; regional PV variation | Autoencoder/decoder and clear-sky index framing | Threat to generic CSI/residual and decoder claims | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solcom.2023.100050 |
| 2023 | Hybrid ultra-short-term PV power forecasting system for deterministic forecasting and uncertainty analysis | Energy; 5-min PV | Dual-path PLSTNet combines short fluctuation and smoothed long-trend paths | Strong threat to simply adding a trend/ramp branch | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2023.129898 |
| 2023 | Short-term photovoltaic power forecasting using meta-learning and NWP-independent LSTM models | Renewable Energy; one-hour PV | Trainable forecast blending conditioned on regimes | Strong threat to simple residual/meta correction | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.renene.2023.118997 |
| 2024 | ModernTCN: A Modern Pure Convolution Structure for General Time Series Analysis | ICLR; general forecasting | Patch embedding and modern convolution blocks | Confirms the current backbone is a baseline, not project novelty | https://openreview.net/pdf?id=vpJMJerXHU |
| 2024 | TimeMixer: Decomposable Multiscale Mixing for Time Series Forecasting | ICLR; general forecasting | Fine-to-coarse seasonal mixing and coarse-to-fine trend mixing; multiple future predictors | Strong threat to a generic coarse-to-fine/multi-resolution decoder | https://openreview.net/pdf?id=3O5YCEWETq |
| 2024 | Deep learning model for short-term PV forecasting based on variational mode decomposition and similar-day clustering | Computers & Electrical Engineering; PV | Mode decomposition, clustering and hybrid deep learning | Strong threat to generic decomposition plus network stacking | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compeleceng.2024.109116 |
| 2024 | Using sky-classification to improve short-term irradiance prediction | Solar Energy; 20-min clear-sky-index forecasting | Sky classes, optical flow, CSI; explicitly notes smoothing and difficulty with changes | Shows missing future cloud-state information can dominate ramps; requires sky imagery unavailable here | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2024.112320 |
| 2024 | MMFNet: Multi-Scale Frequency Masking Neural Network for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting | arXiv; general forecasting | Multi-scale frequency decomposition and learnable masks | Strong threat to generic frequency-domain constraints | https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02070 |
| 2025 | Towards Measuring and Modeling Geometric Structures in Time Series Forecasting via Image Modality | arXiv; general forecasting | Shape-aware temporal loss combines first difference, FFT and perceptual loss | Direct threat to claiming derivative/frequency loss itself as novel | https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23253 |
| 2025 | Robust photovoltaic power output forecasting and ramping capacity estimation for smart energy systems | Array; PV | Enhanced TCN plus invariant risk minimization under weather/domain shift | Shows TCN+ramp applications and distribution robustness are occupied | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.array.2025.100576 |
| 2026 | A decompose-reshape-ensemble deep learning framework for multi-scale short-term photovoltaic power forecasting | Scientific Reports; PV multi-horizon | Decomposition, reshape, ensemble; evaluates Hausdorff and ramp metrics | High novelty threat to another multi-scale/decomposition PV method | https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-53170-7 |
| 2026 | High-resolution long-term photovoltaic power forecasting based on a dual-branch framework | Solar Energy; high-resolution PV | Parallel baseline/ramp branches, ramp-specific loss, residual fusion | Directly blocks a simple “power+ramp branch solves smoothing” novelty claim | https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0038092X26005931 |

Older but methodologically decisive shape-loss precedents were also checked: DILATE (shape and temporal distortion loss, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09020) and TILDE-Q (transformation-invariant loss, https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.15050). Consequently, “add a derivative loss”, “add a shape loss”, “add FFT loss”, “add multi-scale decoder”, and “add a ramp branch” are not defensible stand-alone innovations.
