fig1_design
Caption: Independent fitting and forecast-time information. Every step is five minutes; cumulative windows use the first 12, 48, 96 or 144 future steps.
Alt: Three site boxes feed a common evaluation protocol, not a pooled model. A time axis separates six-hour inputs, origin and twelve-hour targets; Daily uses target minus twenty-four hours.

fig2_alice_references
Caption: Post hoc minimum three-seed mean neural RMSE relative to both references on the same Daily-valid targets. F: full; A: power-active. The envelope is descriptive, not deployable.
Alt: Twenty-four Alice comparisons on shared logarithmic axes: both references use the Daily intersection. Ratio one is the comparison boundary; unfavorable ratios remain visible.

fig3_external_skills
Caption: Prespecified external Inverted-variate skill. Dots are individual seeds; bars show sample SD of three seed metrics, not temporal confidence intervals. Each reference uses its own matched support.
Alt: Four panels show two external sites and two references, with full and power-active curves. NIST twelve-hour full crosses below zero for Daily; no Alice extreme compresses this scale.

fig4_neural_gaps
Caption: Relative RMSE gaps from the best mean neural implementation in each cumulative window and power scope. Zero identifies the lowest error; small gaps are shown as small gaps rather than enlarged rank differences.
Alt: Four model curves show NIST full and power-active winners differing across windows, while Yulara favors Inverted-variate. Axes begin at zero and retain all relative gaps.

fig5_decomposition_case
Caption: Left: additive active/low-power contributions to full MSE on NIST twelve-hour Daily-matched targets; neural contributions are averaged over seeds. Right: one seed42 trajectory from the fixed calendar selection, not a rolling reconstruction.
Alt: Stacked squared-error contributions show active error savings offset by low-power excess. A fixed October origin compares observed, neural and Daily trajectories without claiming a weather event.

figS1_alice_ranks
Caption: Alice neural ranks by cumulative window and target-power scope. F: full; A: power-active; four discrete rank levels.
Alt: Three facets retain co-located arrays separately and show discrete numerical ranks for four models.

figS2_common_origins
Caption: Horizon-specific (HS, solid) versus common-H144-origin (CO, dashed) cumulative errors. Bars are seed SD. Left panels preserve reference error ranges; right panels show neural detail on separately labelled axes.
Alt: Five target rows compare original support with a fixed complete twelve-hour origin set; changes cannot be attributed solely to window length.

figS3_latency
Caption: Historical 17-channel inference timings on RTX 3060 Laptop GPU, excluding loading/preprocessing. Point summaries do not establish a stable difference between 0.535 and 0.558 ms; no new timing experiment.
Alt: A logarithmic latency axis displays all four models and their parameter counts without compressing sub-millisecond values into zero.

figS4_fixed_leads
Caption: Lead-specific error at each five-minute lead on common complete-H144 origins and Daily-valid targets. These are not cumulative-prefix metrics. Neural curves average per-seed RMSE.
Alt: Five system panels show fixed-lead curves on one origin set, distinguishing them from cumulative windows.

figS5_block_intervals
Caption: Paired temporal-block percentile intervals (2000 resamples; 24/48/72 h). SSE/count is aggregated before each seed RMSE, then seed skills are averaged. These intervals are conditional on observed dates and fitted models, not prediction intervals.
Alt: Temporal intervals expose uncertainty around small long-window Daily improvements. All three prechosen block sizes are shown, including intervals crossing zero.

figS6_monthly
Caption: Monthly errors assign each full twelve-hour trajectory to its forecast-origin month on common H144 Daily-valid support. Bars show seed SD.
Alt: October, November and December are compared without assigning months by target time or treating them as independent climate replications.

figS7_cases
Caption: Fixed calendar and explicitly post hoc best/worst seed42 H144 Daily-improvement cases. Each curve is one forecast origin; extremes are diagnostic, not representative frequencies. No weather or outage labels are inferred.
Alt: Ten trajectory panels include predetermined dates plus both favorable and unfavorable error extremes for the same fixed seed.

figS8_ridge
Caption: Post hoc Ridge with recent history and Ridge plus available previous-day trajectory; alphas selected on Validation only. Daily-matched full targets are identical to the neural comparison. Ridge is deterministic; neural bars are three-seed SD.
Alt: Five systems compare simple learned references against Inverted-variate and Daily; all unfavorable results are retained.

figS9_learning_budget
Caption: Historical NIST TCN learning records. All three selected checkpoints occur at the fixed maximum budget of 25 epochs. These curves document the budget boundary, not convergence or a new fit.
Alt: Three historical training and validation curves expose continued improvement at the budget boundary; no extended training was performed.
